# ---- Players page ----
players.title = NHL Players
players.lede = Every regular in the NHL, ranked by Impact per game: the points he adds per game over an average player at his position.
players.note = Impact is a box-score rating: a goal is worth 0.75, a first assist 0.70, a second assist 0.55, a shot on goal 0.075, a blocked shot 0.05, a takeaway 0.03 (a giveaway costs the same), a minor penalty drawn 0.15 (one taken costs the same) and each goal of plus-minus 0.15. A player's total is compared with what an average forward or defenseman would have in the same ice time. It cannot see shot quality or who else was on the ice, and it does not adjust for the opponent. Early in the season a few games can move a player a long way.
players.note_goalies = A goalie's Impact is the goals he saved beyond what an average goalie would have on the same number of shots (at even strength, against the power play and shorthanded), at 0.75 a goal. Impact does not see shot quality; goals saved above expected, on his card, does. Early in the season a game or two can move a goalie a long way.
players.back = All players
players.nhl_link = His page on NHL.com

A line broken up during the game, an injury or a benching shows as it happened. Left and right sides go by each player's listed position and shooting hand, so a center playing wing may be shown in the middle. Choose a player for his card.

odds.note = These are this site's own estimate, not a sportsbook's line. Every coming game is decided by the chance of winning shown on the Games page; the standings, the wild cards and the bracket follow the league's rules, and each series is best of seven. Because nobody knows exactly how good a team is, least of all early in the season, each team's rating is moved up or down by a random amount every time the season is played out.
