# ---- Players page ----
players.title = NHL Players
players.lede = Every regular in the NHL, ranked by Impact per game: the points he adds per game over an average player at his position.
players.note = Impact is a box-score rating: a goal is worth 0.75, a first assist 0.70, a second assist 0.55, a shot on goal 0.075, a blocked shot 0.05, a takeaway 0.03 (a giveaway costs the same), a minor penalty drawn 0.15 (one taken costs the same) and each goal of plus-minus 0.15. A player's total is compared with what an average forward or defenseman would have in the same ice time. It cannot see shot quality or who else was on the ice, and it does not adjust for the opponent. Early in the season a few games can move a player a long way.
players.note_goalies = A goalie's Impact is the goals he saved beyond what an average goalie would have on the same shots (at even strength, against the power play and shorthanded), at 0.75 a goal. It cannot see shot quality. Early in the season a game or two can move a goalie a long way.
players.back = All players
players.nhl_link = His page on NHL.com
